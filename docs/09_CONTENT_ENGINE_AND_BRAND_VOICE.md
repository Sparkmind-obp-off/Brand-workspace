# Content Engine and Brand Voice

## Goal
Create useful, recognizable content from real source material while preserving the owner's identity and business positioning.

## Brand Profile
A brand profile should contain:
- positioning
- audience
- offers
- differentiators
- content pillars
- tone and personality
- vocabulary and phrases
- claims/evidence rules
- topics to avoid
- CTA patterns
- examples of approved content

## Source-of-Truth Hierarchy
1. User-provided facts and source material.
2. Approved brand knowledge.
3. Verified external information where research is explicitly enabled.
4. Model inference only when clearly labeled and low-risk.

The engine must not invent credentials, customers, results, partnerships, or personal experiences.

## Canonical Content Object
A content concept should exist independently of channels and include:
`objective`, `audience`, `core_idea`, `evidence`, `angle`, `hook`, `body`, `cta`, `media_brief`, `risk_level`.

## Channel Adaptation
The engine creates native variants for Instagram, Facebook, and Threads. It should preserve the underlying idea while adapting length, format, tone, CTA, and media requirements.

## Quality Checks
- factual support
- brand consistency
- originality/repetition
- clarity
- useful value
- CTA alignment
- prohibited/risky claims
- platform-specific constraints

## Human Voice
The system should learn from approved and rejected outputs. Rejection reasons become structured feedback instead of merely deleting drafts.
